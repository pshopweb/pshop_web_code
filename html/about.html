Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?
You can track your order by logging into your account and visiting the "My Orders" page. You'll find tracking information for each order.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy on most items. Items must be in their original condition. Contact our support team to initiate a return.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes! We ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. Check our shipping page for details.
How can I contact support?
You can reach us through the Messages page in your account, or email us at support@pshop.com. We respond within 24 hours.
